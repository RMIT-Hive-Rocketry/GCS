Overview

The above mute/unmute (default mute) icon can be used to toggle whether alarms play or not. The alarms themselves signify if something is wrong with connections to external devices (signified by a non-green indicator), and play in a queue from top to bottom priority (GSE, AV, GPS Fix, Dual Board). Since the GPS Fix is part of the AV device, if the former goes down, so will the latter. Click the following button to hear them in succession (0.5 sec of silence between each):

Description of each sound:
Device (sorted by descending order of priority) Alarm sound Example
GSE High-pitched chord
AV 1kHz square wave
GPS Fix ~250 hZ Triangle wave just under 2 octaves below AV
Dual Board Low-pitched sound, slightly ascending
