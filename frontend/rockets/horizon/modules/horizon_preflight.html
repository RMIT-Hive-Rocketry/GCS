Overview

The above mute/unmute (default mute) icon can be used to toggle whether sounds play or not. The 1st 6 are alarms that signify if something is wrong with connections to external devices (signified by a non-green indicator), and play in a queue from top to bottom priority (GSE, AV, GPS Fix, Dual Board), and the last 2 are sounds for specific events. Click the following button to hear them in succession (same order as the below table), with 0.5 sec of silence between each (including any extended alarms):
