Overview

The above mute/unmute (default mute) icon can be used to toggle whether sounds play or not. The 1st 4 sounds (2 of them with quicker versions) are alarms that signify if something is wrong with connections to external devices (signified by a non-green indicator), and play in a queue from top to bottom priority (GSE, AV, GPS Fix), the others being for specific events. Click the following button to hear them in succession (same order as described below), with 0.5 sec of silence between each (including any quicker versions):

Description of each sound:
The GSE loss is a high-pitched chord (and AV loss a 1kHz square wave), both of which play in quicker succession when timed out for more than 5 seconds. The GPS Fix Loss is a triangle wave pitch-shifted to ~0.5 octaves below AV loss. When the rocket reaches Apogee a celebratory piano chord is played, 2 seconds after which (and then at 1200ft) a descending sine sweep is played. In terms of safety, if the rocket is within 50m of the GCS, a horn tritone plays for as long as is the case, increasing to full volume during the 1st iteration (~16 seconds). Currently this covers the case when the rocket is about to hit someone on the head (as this event is not separately detectable)
